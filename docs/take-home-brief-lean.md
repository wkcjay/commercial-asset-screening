# Take-Home — Digital & AI Product Builder

## The situation

ABC Development Group is a regional real estate developer and asset owner — residential, retail, and commercial across Southeast Asia. Its development team assesses potential sites and monitors how existing assets are performing.

Today, that work — pulling together comparable transactions, the supply pipeline, demographics, accessibility, zoning, and then writing it all up into a view someone can act on — is manual, slow, and inconsistent from person to person.

## The brief

**Build something that uses AI to make this faster or better.**

That's the whole brief. You decide what to build, who it's for, what's in scope, what data to use, and what "good" looks like. Those choices are part of what we're assessing.

## What we're assessing

This is a full-stack build, so we're looking across several dimensions at once — you decide how to balance them:

- **Design thinking** — how you frame the problem, choose a user, and scope.
- **Software engineering** — how you build and structure working software.
- **AI engineering** — how you use AI in a grounded, trustworthy way.
- **Data engineering** — how you source, model, and move data.
- **Data analytics** — how you turn data into a genuine insight.
- **Product & domain sense** — whether what you build is something a real estate team would actually trust and use.

You won't have time to max out all of these. Where you invest, and what you trade off, tells us a lot.

## What we'd like back

- **A link to a Git repository** with your code.
- **A clear README** in the repo that explains your solution — how the code is structured and what your key decisions were — and a **step-by-step setup/startup guide** so we can get it running ourselves.
- Come on-site and walk us through it: what you built, why, and what you'd do next.

## A few ground rules

- Budget roughly **6–8 hours**. We'd rather see sharp decisions than a finished product.
- Use any stack, tools, and data you like — real or synthetic.
- We're as interested in what you left out as in what you built.
